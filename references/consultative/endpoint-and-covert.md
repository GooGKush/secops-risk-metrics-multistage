# Consultative Deep-Dive: Endpoint Activity, Living-off-the-Land & Covert Signaling

## 1. The Deterministic Rule Failure Mode
EDR rules are predominantly binary and signature-oriented:
* Example: `process.command_line = "*mimikatz*"`
* Example: `security_result.severity = "CRITICAL"`

**Why Attackers Slip Past**:
1. **Living-off-the-Land (LotL)**: Threat actors execute benign administration tools (`powershell.exe`, `wmic.exe`, `curl.exe`, `mshta.exe`). Since the binary itself is signed by Microsoft or Apple, signature checks pass.
2. **Off-Hours Execution**: Running an administrative utility at 3:00 AM on Sunday is indistinguishable from running it at 2:00 PM on Tuesday in a static rule that only checks process name.
3. **Alert Dilution**: Low-severity alerts (`INFO` or `LOW`) generated by EDR agents are routinely ignored by SOC analysts due to queue fatigue.

## 2. The Behavioral Antidotes

### Strategy A: Off-Hours Adaptive Schedule Tightening (or `HOUR_OF_DAY_PROFILE` Handoff)
* **Target Metric**: `metrics.file_executions_total`
* **Math Model**: `adaptive_context_threshold.yl2` (`hourly_temporal_zscore.yl2` retired per `D1`; per-hour-of-day profiling hands off to `secops-statistical-hunter` `HOUR_OF_DAY_PROFILE`)
* **How It Defeats the Blind Spot**: Evaluates each execution's local hour and weekday in `{{tenant_tz}}`, tightening the significance gate from `3.0σ` in business hours to `1.75σ` during off-hours or weekends.

### Strategy B: Rare Process Prevalence (Derived File Prevalence / Poisson Rarity)
* **Target Metric**: `metrics.file_executions_success`
* **Math Model**: `hybrid_metric_derived_file_prevalence_2stage.yl2`, `hybrid_metric_fleet_prevalence_2stage.yl2`, or `poisson_rarity.yl2` (single-plane `fleet_prevalence_shield.yl2` retired per `D1`)
* **How It Defeats the Blind Spot**: Evaluates both the rarity of the SHA256 binary for that specific host and its global prevalence across the entire organization (`artifact.prevalence.rolling_max <= 3`).

### Strategy C: Stealth Alert Frequency Drift (Longitudinal CUSUM)
* **Target Metric**: `metrics.alert_event_name_count`
* **Math Model**: `longitudinal_cusum.yl2`
* **How It Defeats the Blind Spot**: Accumulates subtle daily counts of low-severity alerts fired on an endpoint over up to 14 days (the multi-stage search limit), detecting the gradual operational footprint of an active adversary before high-severity alerts trip.

## 3. Operational Triage SLA & Chronicle Pivot
When outliers are surfaced:
1. **Command Line & Parent Hierarchy**: Pivot to `principal.process.command_line` and `principal.process.parent_process.file.full_path` to identify script contents and parent execution trees.
2. **Binary Hash Reputation**: Check `principal.process.file.sha256` against VirusTotal and internal deployment repositories.
